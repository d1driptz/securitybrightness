Our approach

Trust should have boundaries.

Giving an application access does not mean approving everything it might do. SecurityBrightness focuses on checking proposed actions against application identity, permissions, policy and — when needed — a deliberate human decision.

Implemented foundation

Authorization engine

A Python core, local service and SDK evaluate registered application requests using policies and scopes.

Prototype

Desktop human review

A Windows-facing prototype supports reviewing proposals, inspecting application authority and reading decision history.

Experimental

Isolated broker research

Separate inactive fixture experiments test stricter lifecycle and resource boundaries. They are not general device protection.

What it does not do today: Simply installing SecurityBrightness does not intercept arbitrary Windows applications or protect every file. Protection depends on cooperating integrations and trusted enforcement points. The experimental broker is not enabled for general application delivery.

Development status

Built step by step. Verified before claims.

SecurityBrightness is actively evolving through implementation, automated testing, and controlled demonstrations. Passing tests support specific behaviours; they do not prove comprehensive real-world protection.
